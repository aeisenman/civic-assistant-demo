## Overview

The City's Environmental Services Department (ESD) collects trash, recycling, and organics
(food scraps and yard waste) for single-family homes and small multi-family properties (generally
4 units or fewer); larger apartment and condo complexes typically contract with a private hauler
instead. Trash and organics are collected every week on your zone's assigned day; recycling is
collected every other week on that same day, and the address lookup tells you which weeks are
yours. Look up your exact collection day and next recycling date at
getitdone.sandiego.gov/CollectionMapLookup by entering your address.

Bins need to be at the curb or in the alley by 6:00 a.m. on your collection day and pulled back in
by 6:00 p.m. the same day. Measure B, passed by voters in 2022, allowed the City to charge for
residential trash collection for the first time in about a century; billing began July 1, 2025 at
roughly $42.76/month for a standard 3-cart service (estimate). The fee is billed on the
county property tax bill, not on the water bill; confirm your exact rate there.

There are five holidays with no collection: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving,
and Christmas. If a holiday falls on a weekday, that day's route -- and every remaining route for
the rest of that week -- shifts one day later; a holiday on a Friday can push that week's Friday
route to Saturday. Check the Trash Service Updates page before assuming a missed pickup is an
error, since holiday shifts and occasional route delays are posted there first.

If your bin genuinely wasn't collected and there's no posted delay covering it, file a Missed
Collection report through Get It Done or call 858-694-7000; ESD generally dispatches missed trash
the next business day. Broken or missing containers, requests for a larger or additional cart, and
bulky item pickups (furniture, appliances -- typically about 2 free pickups per year with an item
cap, estimate) all go through the same Get It Done channel or phone line.

## How it works

1. Look up your address at getitdone.sandiego.gov/CollectionMapLookup to get your collection day
   (trash and organics go out every week) and the date of your next recycling collection.
2. Set bins out at the curb/alley by 6:00 a.m. on your collection day; bring them in by 6:00 p.m.
3. On a suspected miss, check the Trash Service Updates page first for a known holiday shift or
   route delay before assuming a pickup was missed.
4. If nothing is posted, file a Missed Collection report via Get It Done or call 858-694-7000;
   ESD dispatches next business day in the normal case.
5. For a broken/missing container, a size or quantity change, or a bulky item pickup, submit the
   matching request type through Get It Done or the same phone line.

## FAQ

### Q: What day is my trash picked up?
Look up your address at getitdone.sandiego.gov/CollectionMapLookup. Trash and organics are
collected every week on the same day; recycling is collected every other week on that same day,
and the address lookup gives you the date of your next recycling collection.

### Q: Why wasn't my recycling picked up this week?
Recycling is collected every other week, not every week like trash and organics are, so the most
likely answer is that this was your off week. Look up your address to see the date of your next
recycling collection before you report a miss; if that date has already passed without a
collection, file a Missed Collection report through Get It Done or call 858-694-7000.

### Q: What time do bins need to be out?
Bins need to be at the curb or in the alley by 6:00 a.m. on your collection day, and pulled back in
by 6:00 p.m. the same day.

### Q: My trash wasn't picked up -- what do I do?
First check the Trash Service Updates page for a known holiday shift or route delay covering your
area. If nothing is posted, file a Missed Collection report through Get It Done or call
858-694-7000; a crew is typically dispatched the next business day.

### Q: How does the holiday schedule work?
There's no collection on five major holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving,
and Christmas. If one falls on a weekday, that day's route and every remaining route that week
shifts one day later -- so a Friday holiday can push a Friday route to Saturday.

### Q: What can go in recycling?
Clean, dry, loose paper and cardboard, rigid plastics, metal cans, and glass all belong in
recycling. Don't bag your recyclables and keep food-soiled items (like a greasy pizza box) out --
those go in organics instead, since food residue contaminates the recycling stream.

### Q: Can food scraps go in the green bin?
Yes -- food scraps and yard waste both go in the organics (green) bin. Avoid using plastic bags,
even ones labeled "compostable," since most municipal composting facilities can't process them.

### Q: How do I get a bulky item picked up?
Request a bulky item pickup through Get It Done or by calling 858-694-7000. Residents typically get
about 2 free pickups per year with an item cap per visit (estimate) -- verify the current allowance
with ESD since it can change.

### Q: My trash can is broken or missing -- how do I get a new one?
Submit a Repair/Replace or Missing Container request through Get It Done, or call 858-694-7000.
ESD will schedule a replacement or repair; there's no charge for a container that's simply worn out
or damaged in normal use.

### Q: How do I get a bigger or extra trash can?
Property owners can request a size change or an extra container at wasteportal.sandiego.gov or by
calling 858-694-7000. There may be a fee difference depending on the size and number of carts
requested (estimate -- confirm current rates on the portal).

### Q: Why am I being charged a trash fee now?
Measure B, approved by San Diego voters in 2022, allowed the City to start charging for residential
trash collection for the first time in nearly a century. Billing began July 1, 2025, at roughly
$42.76/month for standard 3-cart service (estimate).

### Q: Does the trash fee apply to apartments and condos?
Generally the fee and the City's direct collection service apply to single-family homes and small
multi-family properties (about 4 units or fewer). Larger apartment and condo complexes typically
arrange their own private hauler and aren't on the City's route.
