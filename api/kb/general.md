## Overview

Some of the most common resident questions aren't handled by any single department, and some
aren't handled by the City at all. Library hours vary by branch -- check the specific branch page
or call 619-236-5800, and note that most branches close on City holidays. Water bills are paid
through MyWaterSD online, by phone, by mail, or in person; paying from a linked bank account has no
fee, while a card payment carries roughly a 1.59% fee (estimate). A water leak on the City's side of
the meter (typically the street-facing side) should be reported to the Water Emergency Hotline at
619-515-3525. Parking citations can be paid online with the citation number at
sandiego.gov/treasurer/payments/citation, and payment is due within 21 days to avoid additional
penalties.

Two lookups residents ask about often are entirely self-service: find your council district and
councilmember using the interactive Council District map on the City's site, and find your street
sweeping day and time window using the online street sweeping map (sweeping is typically monthly,
7:00 AM-2:00 PM, though the exact schedule varies by block). This demo does not include a dedicated
street-sweeping lookup tool -- point residents to the map.

A meaningful share of "city" questions are actually handled by the County, the State, or the
federal government, and the City's role is simply to point residents the right direction. Dog
licensing goes through the San Diego Humane Society or County Animal Services, not the City. Voter
registration goes through the County Registrar of Voters at sdvote.com. Property tax payments,
marriage licenses, and passport applications (partially -- some library branches are acceptance
facilities) all sit outside City government too. See the referral data for the complete list of
what to hand off and where.

When in doubt about whether something is a City function, the safest move is to check the referral
list first -- it's much shorter to confirm "this isn't us" than to give a resident the wrong office
to call.

## How it works

1. A resident asks a general question that doesn't map cleanly to permits, business, trash, an
   event, or a 311-style service request.
2. Check whether it's a **City function** the assistant can answer directly from policy text (a
   water bill, a parking ticket, library hours) or a **tool lookup** (council district, in this
   demo).
3. If it's not a City function at all, use the **referral table** to point to the right agency --
   County, State, or Federal -- along with its URL.
4. If it's ambiguous, default to routing the resident toward the most specific department contact
   available rather than a generic city phone number.

## FAQ

### Q: Is the library open today?
Hours vary by branch, so check that branch's page on the City's website or call 619-236-5800 to
confirm. Most branches are closed on City holidays, so double-check around holiday weekends.

### Q: How do I pay my water bill?
Pay online through MyWaterSD, by phone, by mail, or in person. Paying from a linked bank account
has no fee; paying by card carries roughly a 1.59% fee (estimate).

### Q: I have a water leak -- who do I call?
Call the Water Emergency Hotline at 619-515-3525. The City repairs leaks on its side of the meter
(typically the street-facing portion of the line); a leak on your side of the meter is the property
owner's responsibility.

### Q: How do I pay a parking ticket?
Pay online with your citation number at sandiego.gov/treasurer/payments/citation. Payment is due
within 21 days of the citation to avoid additional penalties.

### Q: How do I find my council district?
Enter your address into the interactive Council District map on the City's website to see your
district number and councilmember.

### Q: When is street sweeping on my block?
Enter your address into the online street sweeping map to see your scheduled day and time window.
Sweeping is typically monthly between 7:00 AM and 2:00 PM, though the exact schedule varies block
by block.

### Q: How do I license my dog?
Dog licensing is handled by the San Diego Humane Society or County Animal Services, not the City
directly -- see the referral list for the current link and process.

### Q: How do I register to vote?
Register through the County Registrar of Voters at sdvote.com. Voter registration is a County
function, not something the City administers.

### Q: How do I get a library card, and does it cost anything?
A San Diego Public Library card is free for California residents, for people who own property in
California, and for military personnel stationed in California. A non-resident card costs $32.50 a
year. Apply online or at any branch, with photo identification and proof of your address. The card
types and the application are described at
sandiego.gov/public-library/services/lending/librarycard. Branch hours vary, so check the branch
page or call 619-236-5800 before going in person.

### Q: How do I apply for a job with the City?
City jobs are posted and applied for online only, at sandiego.gov/governmentjobs. Paper and emailed
applications are not accepted, so an application has to go through that site. Questions about a
posting, an application, or the hiring process go to 619-236-6400. Each posting carries its own
filing deadline, so check the closing date on the posting itself rather than assuming a job is
still open.

### Q: A stray dog is running loose on my street -- who do I call?
The San Diego Humane Society is the City's animal control partner and handles stray, loose,
injured and aggressive animals. Call 619-299-7012 extension 1; the line is staffed 24 hours a day.
The City's animal services page is sandiego.gov/park-and-recreation/activities/animalservices.
This is not a City department and it is not a Get It Done report, so do not wait for a City crew.
A dead animal on a public street is a Get It Done report instead. If an animal is actively
attacking someone, call 911.

### Q: My neighbours are playing loud music at 1 a.m. -- what do I do?
Call the Police non-emergency line at 619-531-2000 while the noise is still going, because an
officer has to hear it to act on it. Residential quiet hours run from 10 p.m. to 7 a.m. under San
Diego Municipal Code section 59.5.04. The same line handles construction that starts before the
permitted hour, and a repeated violation can draw a $1,000 administrative citation. The City's
noise complaint page is sandiego.gov/police/community/noise-complaints. Noise is not a Get It Done
category, so a report filed there will not reach an officer.
